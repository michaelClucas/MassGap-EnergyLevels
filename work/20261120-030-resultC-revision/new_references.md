## RDM / SDP / bootstrap lower bounds [new section, or 03-spectral-gap-methods.md]
- Nakata et al., J. Chem. Phys. 114, 8282 (2001). doi:10.1063/1.1360199. Variational 2-RDM via SDP: rigorous lower bounds on E_0.
- Mazziotti, PRL 93, 213001 (2004). doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.213001. Same method.
- Kazakov, Zheng, "Bootstrap for lattice Yang-Mills theory", PRD 107, L051501 (2023). arXiv:2203.11360. Two-sided Wilson-loop bounds, infinite volume, all couplings; observables, not the gap.
- Kazakov, Zheng, finite-N SU(2) bootstrap, JHEP 03 (2025) 099. arXiv:2404.16925. Same.
- Guo, Li, Yang, Zhu, SU(3) lattice YM bootstrap, JHEP 12 (2025) 033. arXiv:2502.14421. Same.
- Nancarrow, Xin, gap bootstrap for the 1+1D TFIM, JHEP 2023, 052. arXiv:2211.03819. Thermodynamic-limit gap lower bound without frustration-freeness.
- Rao, SDP gap certificates for qubit Hamiltonians (2025). arXiv:2510.08427. Convergent hierarchy, no frustration-freeness or geometric locality needed; finite local dimension.
- Scheer, Khalaf, exact lower bounds on the Laughlin gap (2026). arXiv:2610.18854. SDP gap certificate in the thermodynamic limit for an FF FQH model.

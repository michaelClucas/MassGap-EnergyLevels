# 08. Existing glueball models (Result-B competitors)

Tags as in `README.md`. Parameter counts below are approximate and must be rechecked from the papers before any Result-B comparison.

**Isgur, Paton, "Flux-tube model for hadrons in QCD", Phys. Rev. D 31, 2910 (1985).** DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.31.2910 [Crossref]
- Strong-coupling Hamiltonian lattice picture → flux tubes; glueballs as closed flux loops (rings with phonon excitations). Input: string tension and a tube/loop size parameter.
- Setting: model, 3+1D, SU(3).
- Relevance: derived from Hamiltonian LGT at strong coupling; closest existing model to line 1. Closed-string spectrum vs lattice (Athenodorou-Teper) is the comparison.

**Jaffe, Johnson, "Unconventional states of confined quarks and gluons", Phys. Lett. B 60, 201 (1976).** DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(76)90423-8 [Crossref]
- MIT bag model glueballs: gluon cavity modes (TE/TM) in a spherical bag; glueball = bag with two or three gluon modes.
- Relevance: literally a "particle in a box" bound-state model. Parameters: bag constant B, plus corrections (zero-point, coupling).

**Szczepaniak, Swanson, Ji, Cotanch, PRL 76, 2011 (1996)** arXiv:hep-ph/9511422 [arXiv-API]; **Szczepaniak, Swanson, PRD 65, 025012 (2001)** arXiv:hep-ph/0107078 [arXiv-API]
- Coulomb-gauge Hamiltonian, BCS vacuum, quasi-gluons with dynamical mass, TDA/RPA glueballs. Parameters: string tension and a coupling / cutoff. (See file 06.)

**Llanes-Estrada, Cotanch, Bicudo, Ribeiro, "QCD Glueball Regge Trajectories and the Pomeron", Nucl. Phys. A 710, 45 (2002).** arXiv:hep-ph/0008212 [arXiv-API]
- Same Coulomb-gauge many-body framework applied to glueball Regge trajectories.

**Mathieu, Buisseret, Semay, "Gluons in glueballs: Spin or helicity?", Phys. Rev. D 77, 114022 (2008).** arXiv:0802.0088 [arXiv-API]
- Two-gluon constituent (potential) model with Jacob-Wick helicity formalism; massless helicity-1 constituents reproduce lattice J^PC without spurious states.
- Relevance: constituent-gluon "atom" model; a two-body Schrödinger/spinless-Salpeter bound-state spectrum, i.e. the most literal "electron level" glueball model already in print.

**Mathieu, Kochelev, Vento, "The Physics of Glueballs", Int. J. Mod. Phys. E 18, 1 (2009).** arXiv:0810.4453 [arXiv-API]
- Review covering bag, flux-tube, constituent, QCD sum rules, lattice, AdS/QCD.

**Witten, "Anti-de Sitter Space, Thermal Phase Transition, And Confinement In Gauge Theories", Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2, 505 (1998).** arXiv:hep-th/9803131 [arXiv-API]
**Csáki, Ooguri, Oz, Terning, "Glueball Mass Spectrum From Supergravity", JHEP 9901:017 (1999).** arXiv:hep-th/9806021 [arXiv-API]
**Brower, Mathur, Tan, "Glueball Spectrum for QCD from AdS Supergravity Duality", Nucl. Phys. B 587, 249 (2000).** arXiv:hep-th/0003115 [arXiv-API]
- Glueball masses = normalizable modes of supergravity fields in the AdS black-hole geometry: a 1D Schrödinger (Sturm-Liouville) eigenvalue problem. One scale parameter; ratios predicted.
- Setting: holographic dual of a theory in the YM universality class only at strong 't Hooft coupling with extra KK modes; not pure YM.
- Relevance: **the holographic gap is literally a bound-state problem in a 1D effective potential.** Any "Schrödinger equation in an effective potential gives glueball ratios" Result-B model must beat these one-parameter fits.

**Leigh, Minic, Yelnikov, arXiv:hep-th/0604060** (file 04): 2+1D analytic spectrum from Bessel zeros; essentially zero free parameters beyond the coupling; claims excellent lattice agreement in 2+1D. A strong Result-B competitor in 2+1D.

**KKN string tension (file 04)** and **Isgur-Paton** give the 2+1D and 3+1D m/√σ benchmarks.
